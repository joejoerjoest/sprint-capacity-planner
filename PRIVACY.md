# Privacy Policy — Sprint Capacity Planner for Jira

Last updated: June 11, 2026

## Data Collection
Sprint Capacity Planner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data 
outside of your Atlassian environment.

## Data Storage
All data entered into the app (team member names, sprint dates, leave information) 
is stored locally within your Atlassian Jira instance using Forge Storage. 
No data is sent to external servers.

## Third Parties
This app does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.
